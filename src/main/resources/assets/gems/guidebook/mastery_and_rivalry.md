# Mastery and rivals

## Mastery

Successful casts build mastery for the gem you used. At 100 uses, you unlock its first title; at 500, its second. Titles change how your name looks, not your strength.

Press M to see your titles and progress. Choose one to wear, or clear your choice. With no choice set, your current leaderboard title appears if you have one; otherwise, you show the highest title unlocked for your active gem.

Your title appears in chat and the player list. Its color also colors your chat text and, when you have no chosen aura color, your aura.

## Server titles

The server awards temporary titles for leading in kills, low deaths after at least one kill, maximum hearts, 10 energy, synergies, casts and damage. It also tracks kills with each gem. Standings update about once a minute.

## Rivals

When you spawn, another online player may become your rival. Their name appears on your HUD. By default, you deal 25% more damage to them. Defeat them to draw a new rival.

Trust does not stop someone from becoming your rival. The assignment stays across logins; when you are alone on the server, no rival appears.
