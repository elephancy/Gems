# Progression

Your energy decides which gem powers you can use. You start at 3.

## What each level gives you

- **0:** no gem powers.
- **1:** all passives, but no abilities yet.
- **2:** your first ability.
- **3:** your second ability.
- **4:** your third ability.
- **5:** all remaining abilities.
- **6-10:** no new gem abilities. This extra energy gives you a buffer if you die.

Defeat a player who has energy to gain 1. Any death costs 1. Energy stays between 0 and 10. An Energy Upgrade also adds 1, unless you are already at 10.

At 10, your gem glints. Press B to choose bonus powers. A player you defeat who has energy drops an Energy Upgrade. If you fall below 10, your bonus choices return to the shared pool.

## Hearts and gem items

- A Heart raises your maximum health, up to 20 hearts. Death can drop one. The server sets the minimum, which is 5 hearts by default.
- You cannot use a Heart dropped by a player you trust.
- A Gem Trader swaps your active gem. Keep that gem item in your inventory while trading.
- A Gem Purchase Token adds a gem to your collection and makes it active.

You receive your starting gem once; respawning does not give you another. Gem and legendary recipes appear in your recipe book when you join. Players also drop their heads on death.
